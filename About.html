Get to know us

An RMI approved workshop you can trust

AutoRMI is a workshop and spare parts supplier based at 241 Lutting Street, Pretoria West. We are RMI approved, which means we work to the standards of the Retail Motor Industry organisation.

We make sure our clients leave happy, with a car that feels brand new. Our staff never disappoint, so you can always trust them with your car.

Parts, repairs and engine overhauls

  • We supply mechanical, electrical, panel, glass and trim parts.
  • We specialise in engine overhauls and repair engines on all makes and models.
  • We offer repaint and panel work, and can source the parts you need.

Browse our parts or send us an enquiry.

Simple, honest service

  • Free and instant quotes.
  • Same day pickup, with delivery available.
  • Paid on the spot: online payment or cash.
  • Quality, affordable parts to keep your car running for a long time.
